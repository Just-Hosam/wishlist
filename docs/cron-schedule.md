# Cron Schedule

Scheduler: https://cron-job.org/en/

## Update Prices

- Endpoint: `/api/cron/update-prices`
- Schedule: Daily at 4am and 1pm MST

## Update Recommended

- Endpoint: `/api/cron/update-recommended`
- Schedule: Daily at 5am MST

## PlayStation Plus Monthly Games

- Endpoint: `/api/cron/notifications/ps-plus-monthly-games`
- Schedule: Every Tuesday at 6am MST

The scheduler configuration is managed outside this repository.
